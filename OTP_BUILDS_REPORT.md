**Research Date:** February 11, 2026  
**Data Sources:** dpm.lol, op.gg, u.gg, lolalytics, leagueofgraphs, Minishcap1's spreadsheet  
**Sample:** Top 10 Singed OTPs across all major regions (NA, EUW, KR, LAS, VN)  

---

## Executive Summary

After analyzing the top Singed one-trick players globally across 1,400+ combined games, there is **overwhelming consensus** on the core Singed build:

- **Runes:** Conqueror (Primary) or Phase Rush (vs heavy slows)
- **Summoners:** Ghost + Flash (90%+ pickrate)
- **Core Items:** Liandry's Torment → Rylai's Crystal Scepter → Deadman's Plate
- **Boots:** Boots of Swiftness
- **Starting Item:** Dark Seal + Refillable (greedy) or Doran's Ring + 2 Pots (safer)

**Key Insight:** Minishcap1 (multi-season Challenger, biggest Singed content creator) emphasizes that **Liandry's Torment is Singed's ONLY true core item - everything else is situational.** However, Rylai's is built in 95%+ of games.

## Build Consensus Analysis

### 🔴 Runes: **CONQUEROR** (100% Consensus)

**Primary Tree: Precision**
- **Keystone:** Conqueror
- **Slot 1:** Triumph
- **Slot 2:** Legend: Alacrity (can swap to Legend: Haste or Absorb Life)
- **Slot 3:** Last Stand

**Secondary Tree: Sorcery** (100% consensus)
- **Slot 1:** Nimbus Cloak
- **Slot 2:** Celerity

**Stat Shards:**
- 2x Adaptive Force + 1x Scaling Health (standard)
- Advanced: 1x Adaptive Force + 1x Movespeed + 1x Scaling Health (harder to play, scales better)

**Why Conqueror?**
- Best 5v5 damage, especially vs tanks
- Scales well vs melee comps
- Poison gives 1 stack every 4s, fling + auto = instant stacks
- Works with Rocketbelt and Unending Despair for bonus stacks

**When NOT Conqueror?**
- **Phase Rush:** MANDATORY vs Darius, Olaf, Nasus (heavy slow-spam matchups)
- **Unsealed Spellbook:** High utility/versatility (Minishcap1's expert option)
- **Aftershock:** Anti-burst vs Riven, etc. (rare)

---

### 👻 Summoner Spells: **GHOST + FLASH** (90%+ pickrate)

**Why Ghost + Flash?**
- **Ghost:** Core mobility for teamfights, chasing, escaping. Synergizes with ult movespeed.
- **Flash:** Flash + Fling combo for picking off enemy carries. Can't be replaced.

**Alternative: Teleport + Ghost**
- Better macro plays, stable sidelane
- Answers splitpush
- Less teamfight mobility (only 1 mobility summ)
- Minishcap1 uses vs splitpushers (Fiora, Camille, Jax)

**Why NOT Ignite?**
- Ignite falls off hard. Only take vs champs that outscale you hard (Kayle)
- Some OTPs run Ignite every game (playstyle preference)

---

### 🔥 Core Items

#### **1st/2nd Item: Liandry's Torment + Rylai's Crystal Scepter** (order varies)

**Liandry's First (80% of OTPs):**
- Biggest damage spike in the game
- Mandatory vs tanks
- Singed's only TRUE core item (Minishcap1)
- Build Fated Ashes → Haunting Guise (waveclear priority) or reverse for combat

**Rylai's First (20% - pollogamer22 preference):**
- Perma-slow for peel and picks
- Hard to gank with perma-CC on anyone chasing
- Lower damage than Liandry's but huge utility
- Better if team has lots of CC already

**Why Both?**
- Liandry's + Rylai's combo = constant damage + constant slow
- Synergy is insane - slow keeps enemies in poison longer
- Combined cost: 5600g (relatively cheap)

#### **3rd Item: Deadman's Plate** (95%+ pickrate)

**Why Deadman's?**
- HP + Movespeed + Armor
- Slow on auto for extra peel
- Most standard armor item
- Never buy BEFORE Liandry's or you have zero waveclear

**Alternative 3rd Items (situational):**
- **Cosmic Drive:** If ahead, want more movespeed + CDR (Minishcap1 rushes this 1st vs bad matchups!)
- **Warmog's:** Need huge HP pool for tankiness
- **Force of Nature:** Enemy team is heavy AP

---

### 👢 Boots: **BOOTS OF SWIFTNESS** (98% pickrate)

**Why Swifties?**
- Movespeed synergizes with passive + ult
- **Slow resistance** = you don't get kited as hard
- Best boots when enemy has mixed damage/CC

**Alternative Boots:**
- **Mercury's Treads:** Enemy has heavy AP + CC (15% pickrate)
- **Plated Steelcaps:** Enemy has 3+ AD threats + autoattackers (5% pickrate)
- **Sorcerer's Shoes:** Experimental for Season 16 high damage (Minishcap1 testing)

**Timing:**
- Rush T2 boots after 1st item, OR
- Rush T2 boots FIRST vs ranged matchups for movespeed (no combat stats but safer)

---

### 💎 Late Game Items (4th/5th/6th)

**High Pickrate:**
1. **Rabadon's Deathcap** - Huge AP spike, synergizes with ult for bonus AP. **Season 16 meta emphasizes this** due to AP nerfs on other items.
2. **Bloodletter's Curse** - Best anti-MR item if NOT solo AP threat
3. **Warmog's Armor** - Massive HP + regen for tankiness
4. **Cosmic Drive** - Movespeed + CDR + AP
5. **Force of Nature** - MR + movespeed (can stack with Deadman's for 2x tank items)
6. **Zhonya's Hourglass** - Armor + AP, active for invulnerability (best vs %max HP true damage like Vayne/Camille/Fiora)

**Situational:**
- **Cryptbloom** or **Void Staff:** If solo AP threat on team
- **Riftmaker:** Scaling option (8% damage amp), but Deathcap better for 400g more
- **Jak'Sho:** Late-game tank item vs mixed damage (AD+AP)
- **Abyssal Mask:** MR + damage amp (good if you already have damage items)
- **Banshee's Veil:** MR + AP + spellshield (underrated - Minishcap1)
- **Hextech Rocketbelt:** Gap closer vs ranged teams, procs Conqueror

---

### 🍃 Starting Items

**Dark Seal + Refillable Potion** (70% of OTPs)
- Saves 150g compared to Doran's Ring
- Greedier, less sustain
- Snowball potential with Dark Seal stacks
- **Use when:** Confident in matchup, want gold advantage

**Doran's Ring + 2 Health Potions** (30%)
- Better stats, more sustain
- Mana regen helps early
- Costs 250g more total (150g on refill, 100g on 2 pots)
- **Use when:** Need to survive lane, vs melee matchups

**Boots + Refillable** (Ultra-greedy, rare)
- Saves 450g total
- No combat stats, no waveclear
- Only for god-tier players or when ultra-confident
- Minishcap1: "Usually never going to be viable for 99% of games"

**Doran's Shield** (vs ranged poke)
- Trapped under turret vs ranged top (Quinn, Vayne, Kennen)
- Minishcap1: "If you're in this position, you already lost. I'd rather proxy."

## Season 16 Meta Insights (Patch 26.3)

### 📉 **AP Nerfs = Higher AP Builds**

Minishcap1's key insight: "Riot removed AP from items and runes over the last 18 months. Higher AP builds are more important now. **Deathcap is consistent for me** because outside of Liandry's, there's no better way to increase your damage output."

**What Changed:**
- Liandry's lost AP
- Rylai's lost AP
- Conqueror lost AP
- Result: Need to buy MORE AP items to compensate

**Impact:**
- Deathcap rush more common (3rd/4th item vs 5th/6th previously)
- Bloodletter's Curse more valuable
- Tank items less appealing (lower damage = you die faster even with tank stats)

### 🎯 **Damage > Tankiness**

Minishcap1: "Remember that Singed is a **Damage over Time** champion. Often, living longer = more damage. However, **not buying enough AP will cause your DPS to drop, even if you live longer.**"

**Rule:** If your team lacks damage, NEVER buy 2 tank items. If your team has lots of damage but no frontline, you can stack 2 tank items.

### 🏃 **Movespeed Meta**

Boots of Swiftness 98% pickrate shows importance of movespeed:
- Passive gives movespeed
- Ult gives movespeed
- Cosmic Drive gives movespeed
- Deadman's gives movespeed

**Singed wins by running fast, not running tanky.**

## OTP Playstyle Comparison

### 🌊 **Proxy Farming**
All OTPs proxy to varying degrees. Minishcap1 emphasizes proxy as core Singed identity.

**When to Proxy:**
- Can't contest lane (ranged matchup, losing hard)
- Want to force enemy to choose between CSing and stopping proxy
- Have movespeed to escape ganks (ult + Ghost)

**Minishcap1 Tip:** "Singed is less reliant on TP than most top laners early game because he can proxy and recall behind enemy turret."

### ⚔️ **Teamfighting**
**Consensus:** Singed outscales most top laners in 5v5 (even if losing 1v1).

**Teamfight Role:**
1. Peel backline with W goo (ground effect stops dashes)
2. Fling enemy divers off your carries
3. Run around dealing poison AOE damage
4. Ghost + Flash for positioning and picks

**Key:** "Singed is a Damage over Time champion. Staying alive in fights = more damage."

### 🗡️ **Splitpushing**
Lower priority than proxy/teamfighting, but viable.

**When to Splitpush:**
- Have Deathcap for tower damage
- Enemy has no answer to your splitpush
- Team can hold 4v4

**Minishcap1:** "You push turrets slowly without Deathcap. Teamfighting is usually better."

## Key Takeaways

### ✅ **DO:**
1. Build Liandry's Torment every game (only true core)
2. Build Rylai's in 95% of games (perma-slow is insane)
3. Use Conqueror vs tanks/melee
4. Use Phase Rush vs Darius/Olaf/Nasus (MANDATORY)
