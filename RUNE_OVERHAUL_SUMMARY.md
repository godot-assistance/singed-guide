## 🔧 Keystone Distribution & Strategic Reasoning

### Conqueror (22 matchups - 45%)
**Used when:** Extended trades, scaling matchups, enemy can't punish stacking

**Examples:**
- **Cho'Gath:** Free stacking, he can't punish
- **Garen:** Extended trades where poison outdamages his passive
- **Tahm Kench:** Farm lane, need damage to penetrate his HP pool

**Why NOT Phase Rush:** These enemies either lack CC chains, have low mobility, or you can afford to fight them

---

### Phase Rush (18 matchups - 37%)
**Used when:** MUST escape CC, kiting required, execute threat, mobility matchup

**Strategic Choices:**
- **Darius:** Only way to escape E pull + 5-stack passive
- **Illaoi:** MANDATORY to escape her R (instant loss if you stay)
- **Renekton:** Escape his E-W-Q-E combo
- **Sett:** Dodge his W true damage center line
- **Camille:** Escape after she lands E stun
- **K'Sante:** Escape his All Out mode combo
- **Irelia:** Escape when she stacks passive
- **Aatrox:** Escape after he lands Q1 to prevent Q2+Q3 follow-up

**Key Insight:** Phase Rush isn't about "movement speed" - it's about **surviving abilities that would otherwise kill you**

---

### Grasp of the Undying (5 matchups - 10%)
**Used when:** Can't duel late game, need sustain, quick trades only

**Examples:**
- **Pantheon:** Can't fight early, Grasp = quick trade for permanent HP
- **Tryndamere:** Can't duel post-6, Grasp = quick fling then run
- **Fiora:** Can't duel late, Grasp = quick trades only
- **Urgot:** Quick fling trades, HP stacking counters his %HP damage
- **Gangplank:** Sustain vs poke, quick trades when he's melee

**Philosophy:** When you can't win extended fights, make every trade COUNT with permanent HP gain

---

### Unsealed Spellbook (3 matchups - 6%)
**Used when:** Proxy-focused, flexibility needed, summoner swapping wins lane

**Examples:**
- **Nasus:** Start Ghost+Flash → Swap to TP+Smite for double proxy → Back to Ghost+Ignite for kills
- **Sion:** Perfect proxy matchup, swap summoners for maximum chaos

**Why it works:** These matchups are about DENYING farm, not fighting. Summoner flexibility = better map control

---

### Fleet Footwork (3 matchups - 6%)
**Used when:** Ranged cancer, need sustain + movement speed, can't fight

**Examples:**
- **Gnar:** Sustain from his poke + MS to dodge boomerangs
- **Kennen:** Sustain from his harass + MS to dodge skillshots
- **Teemo:** Sustain through blind poke + MS to dodge shrooms

**Why not Grasp:** Fleet gives BOTH sustain AND movement in a single rune - perfect vs ranged poke

---

### Arcane Comet (1 matchup - 2%)
**Used when:** Enemy is stationary, poke-focused lane

**Example:**
- **Malphite:** He's immobile, Comet guarantees harass every Q+poison

**Unique reasoning:** Why Conqueror doesn't make sense here - Malphite never lets you stack it. Comet = guaranteed damage.

---

### Aftershock (2 matchups - 4%)
**Used when:** Enemy has burst combo, need defenses when you CC them

**Examples:**
- **Riven:** When you flip her, Aftershock gives +70 armor/MR, negating her combo
- **Yone:** When you flip him, Aftershock absorbs his burst

**Key mechanic:** Your flip (E) procs Aftershock → You become tank for 2.5s → Survive their burst

## 🎨 Secondary Tree Diversity (Major Improvement)

### Before:
- 80% of matchups: Sorcery (Nimbus Cloak + Celerity)
- Minimal variety, no strategic depth

### After:

#### Resolve Secondary (40% of matchups)
**Used for:** Lane sustain, tankiness, surviving poke/burst
- **Second Wind:** Vs poke (Darius, Renekton, Pantheon, Garen)
- **Conditioning:** Vs AD scalers (Riven, Jax, Yasuo)
- **Bone Plating:** Vs burst (Renekton, Camille, Irelia)
- **Overgrowth:** HP scaling (Gwen, Urgot, Mundo)
- **Unflinching:** CC reduction (paired with tenacity stacks)

#### Inspiration Secondary (30% of matchups)
**Used for:** Economic advantage, utility, sustain combos
- **Magical Footwear:** Free 300g boots (proxy matchups)
- **Perfect Timing:** Free Stopwatch vs divers (Gragas, Fiora, Tristana)
- **Time Warp Tonic:** Sustain amplifier (Gangplank, Malphite)
- **Future's Market:** Earlier item spikes (Nasus denial, Sion proxy)
- **Cosmic Insight:** More summoner spell uptime
- **Approach Velocity:** Close gap when you W slow them (Tryndamere, Jax)

#### Domination Secondary (15% of matchups)
**Used for:** Bonus damage, vision control, ultimate CDR
- **Cheap Shot:** Extra damage on W slow (Mundo, Maokai, Volibear, Shen)
- **Ultimate Hunter:** More R uptime = more built-in 40% Grievous Wounds (Mundo, Volibear, Warwick)
- **Zombie Ward:** Vision control (Kayle, Shen, Sylas)
- **Relentless Hunter:** Out-of-combat MS for roaming (Maokai)

#### Precision Secondary (10% of matchups)
**Used for:** Tenacity stacking, sustain, damage amp
- **Triumph:** Healing in fights
- **Legend: Tenacity:** CC reduction (paired with Tenacity shard + Unflinching)
- **Legend: Alacrity:** Proxy clear speed (Cho'Gath, Malphite, Nasus)
- **Presence of Mind:** Mana sustain for extended proxy (Malphite, Sion)

#### Sorcery Secondary (5% of matchups)
**Used for:** When primary is Precision/Resolve, need escape tools
- Mostly disappeared in favor of more strategic choices

## 🎯 Minor Rune Shard Customization (Game-Changing)

### Before:
- 90% of matchups: Attack Speed + Adaptive Force + Health
- No thought, no strategy

### After:

#### Shard 1 (Offense):
- **Adaptive Force:** When primary tree is Sorcery/Resolve (can't take Attack Speed)
- **Attack Speed:** When primary tree is Precision (auto-attack reset on fling)

#### Shard 2 (Flex):
- **Armor:** Vs AD champions (Darius, Riven, Jax, Yasuo, Renekton)
- **Magic Resist:** Vs AP champions (Gwen, Gragas, Akali, Rumble, Kennen)
- **Adaptive Force:** When damage type is mixed or in easy matchups

#### Shard 3 (Defense):
- **Health Scaling:** Default for most matchups
- **Tenacity:** Vs CC-heavy champions (Darius, Sett, Maokai, Warwick, K'Sante)
- **Double Health Scaling:** Vs true damage (Vayne - since armor is useless)

**Example: Darius Matchup**
- Shard 2: Armor (he does physical damage)
- Shard 3: Tenacity (his W slow + passive is deadly)
- Result: 35% tenacity reduction from shards alone

**Example: Vayne Matchup**
- Shard 2: Health Scaling (her W is true damage, armor useless)
- Shard 3: Health Scaling (double HP stacking)
- Result: Stack as much HP as possible since armor doesn't help

## 🆕 New Rune Synergies Discovered

### 1. **Ultimate Hunter + Built-in Grievous Wounds**
**Matchups:** Mundo, Volibear, Warwick
**Logic:** Your R provides 40% GW → More R uptime = more antiheal windows
**Impact:** 25 second CD reduction = 40% more antiheal uptime

### 2. **Approach Velocity + W Slow**
**Matchups:** Tryndamere, Jax, Mordekaiser
**Logic:** When you W slow them, you get +15% MS TOWARD them → Close gap to fling → Then run away
**Impact:** Makes it easier to land fling then escape

### 3. **Bone Plating vs Burst Champions**
**Matchups:** Renekton, Camille, Irelia
**Logic:** Reduces first 3 instances of damage by 30-60 → Counters burst combos perfectly
**Impact:** Survival vs all-in champions

### 4. **Nullifying Orb + Low HP Scenarios**
**Matchups:** Sett (W magic damage), Rumble, Mordekaiser
**Logic:** Gives magic shield when below 30% HP → Saves you from execute damage
**Impact:** Turns lethal situations into survivable ones

### 5. **Cut Down + HP-Stacking Tanks**
**Matchups:** Ornn, Tahm Kench, Urgot
**Logic:** Enemy stacks 4000+ HP, you have 2500 HP → 15% bonus damage to them
**Impact:** Tank-shredding damage amplification

### 6. **Coup de Grace + Early Kill Pressure**
**Matchups:** Kayle
**Logic:** You MUST kill her pre-6 → Coup de Grace gives execute damage for early kills
**Impact:** Higher success rate on all-ins
