# Quick Reference - Top Changes by Matchup

## 🌟 Most Innovative Rune Changes

### 1. **Malphite: Conqueror → Arcane Comet**
**Why:** He's immobile, you poke him repeatedly. Comet guarantees damage every Q+poison.
**Secondary:** Precision (Presence of Mind for mana, Alacrity for proxy speed)
**Philosophy:** Poke/proxy page, not all-in page

---

### 2. **Nasus: Standard → Unsealed Spellbook**
**Why:** Maximum proxy chaos with summoner swapping
**Strategy:** Ghost+Flash → TP+Smite (double proxy) → Ghost+Ignite (kills)
**Philosophy:** Deny every minion through summoner flexibility

---

### 3. **Mundo: Standard Conqueror → Conqueror + Domination**
**Why:** Ultimate Hunter gives +25s R CDR = more built-in 40% GW uptime
**Unique:** Cheap Shot procs on W slow for bonus damage
**Philosophy:** Optimize around Singed's kit interactions

---

### 4. **Jax: Conqueror → Phase Rush + Approach Velocity**
**Why:** Can't duel late game, need escape when he has R
**Synergy:** W slow triggers Approach Velocity → Run toward him → Fling → Run away faster
**Philosophy:** Win early, escape late

---

### 5. **Pantheon: Conqueror → Full Resolve (Grasp Primary)**
**Why:** Can't fight normally vs early bully
**Strategy:** Quick fling trades for permanent HP (Grasp)
**Philosophy:** When you can't duel, make every trade count

---

### 6. **Sett: Phase Rush → Phase Rush + Nullifying Orb**
**Why:** His W does MAGIC damage, Nullifying Orb gives magic shield at low HP
**Strategy:** Dodge W true damage center with Phase Rush, survive sides with shield
**Philosophy:** Understanding ability damage types matters

---

### 7. **Vayne: Standard → HP Stacking (Double HP Shards)**
**Why:** Her W is true damage, armor is useless
**Strategy:** Stack HP instead of armor
**Philosophy:** Creative adaptation to unique threats

---

### 8. **Gnar/Kennen/Teemo: Standard → Fleet Footwork**
**Why:** Sustain from poke + MS to dodge skillshots in one rune
**Better than:** Grasp (only procs in melee) or Phase Rush (no sustain)
**Philosophy:** Matchup category optimization

---

### 9. **Riven/Yone: Conqueror → Aftershock**
**Why:** When you flip them, Aftershock gives +70 armor/MR for 2.5s
**Strategy:** Absorb their burst combo, then run away
**Philosophy:** Turn their engage against them

---

### 10. **Urgot: Standard → Grasp + HP Stacking**
**Why:** His damage scales with YOUR max HP
**Counter:** Stack HP (Grasp + Overgrowth + HP shards + Heartsteel)
**Philosophy:** Paradoxical counter - more HP makes you tankier vs %HP damage

## 🛡️ Secondary Tree Breakdown

### Resolve (40% of matchups)
**Use when:** Need lane sustain, tankiness, survive poke/burst
- **Second Wind:** Vs poke (Darius, Garen, Pantheon, Renekton)
- **Bone Plating:** Vs burst (Renekton, Camille, Irelia)
- **Conditioning:** Vs scalers (Riven, Jax, Yasuo)
- **Overgrowth:** HP stacking (Gwen, Urgot, Mundo)

### Inspiration (30% of matchups)
**Use when:** Want economy, utility, sustain combos
- **Magical Footwear:** Free 300g (proxy matchups)
- **Perfect Timing:** Free Stopwatch (vs divers)
- **Time Warp Tonic:** Sustain amplifier
- **Cosmic Insight:** More summoner uptime

### Domination (15% of matchups)
**Use when:** Want bonus damage, vision, ult CDR
- **Cheap Shot:** Extra damage on W slow
- **Ultimate Hunter:** More R uptime = more built-in 40% GW
- **Zombie Ward:** Vision control

### Precision (10% of matchups)
**Use when:** Need tenacity, sustain, damage
- **Legend: Tenacity:** CC reduction
- **Legend: Alacrity:** Proxy clear speed
- **Presence of Mind:** Mana sustain for proxy

## 🔥 Unique Rune Synergies

### Ultimate Hunter + Built-in GW
**Matchups:** Mundo, Volibear, Warwick
**Logic:** More R uptime = more 40% GW uptime
**Impact:** 25s CD reduction = 40% more antiheal

### Approach Velocity + W Slow
**Matchups:** Tryndamere, Jax
**Logic:** W slow → +15% MS toward them → Fling → Run away
**Impact:** Makes engage→escape combos smoother

### Bone Plating vs Burst
**Matchups:** Renekton, Camille, Irelia
**Logic:** Reduces first 3 damage instances by 30-60
**Impact:** Survives all-in combos

### Nullifying Orb + Low HP
**Matchups:** Sett, Rumble, Mordekaiser
**Logic:** Magic shield at 30% HP saves from execute
**Impact:** Turns lethal into survivable

### Cut Down + HP Tanks
**Matchups:** Ornn, Tahm Kench, Urgot
**Logic:** They have 4000+ HP, you have 2500 → 15% bonus damage
**Impact:** Tank shredding

## 📈 Tenacity Stacking Strategy (Hard CC Matchups)

### Maximum Tenacity Setup (Darius, Warwick, Maokai):
1. **Legend: Tenacity** (30% at max stacks)
2. **Unflinching** (10% base, up to 20% when low HP)
3. **Tenacity Shard** (10%)
4. **Mercury Treads** (30%)

**Total:** 60%+ tenacity → Reduces 1.5s stun to 0.6s

## 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. **Ult Grievous Wounds**
Your R provides 40% GW while active → Still build Oblivion Orb for lane pressure before teamfights

### 2. **Phase Rush Timing**
Procs after 3 attacks/spells → AA + Fling + Q = instant activation

### 3. **W Ground Mechanic**
Stops dashes → Crucial vs Ambessa, Yasuo, Irelia

### 4. **Proxy Escape Routes**
Always know 2+ escape paths before starting proxy

### 5. **Grasp Quick Trade**
Run in → Fling (Grasp procs) → W slow → Run away with permanent HP
