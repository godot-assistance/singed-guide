## MAJOR FINDINGS

### 1. Core Build is WRONG for Many Matchups

**Aggregate data consensus (Mobalytics, MetaSRC, u.gg):**
The highest WR general build is:
> Liandry's Torment → Boots of Swiftness → Rylai's Crystal Scepter → **Protoplasm Harness** → **Jak'Sho, The Protean**

NOT Liandry's → Rylai's → Deadman's Plate as listed in most matchups.

**Key item changes:**
- **Protoplasm Harness** is now a standard 4th item (not situational) — 600 HP, 15 AH, Lifeline heal scales with armor/MR (synergizes with Singed R). Even after 26.3 nerf (250% → 175% ratio), still turbo good.
- **Jak'Sho, The Protean** replaces Deadman's Plate as the default tank item in many builds. Provides both armor + MR + Voidborn Resilience passive that scales with resists.
- **Riftmaker** is a legitimate 5th/6th item option (60.6% WR per MetaBot, 18% pick rate)
- **Boots of Swiftness** are the default boot choice (54% WR, 68% pickrate per Mobafire), NOT Plated Steelcaps/Mercury Treads

### 3. Summoner Spells — Ignite Is More Viable Than Listed

**Minishcap1 explicitly states** Ignite Singed is viable in S16 because top laners get a FREE Teleport at ~14 min from the top lane quest. This means:
- Ghost + Ignite is viable for aggressive kill lanes (not just Akshan/Kayle/Quinn)
- Flash can even be dropped for Ghost + Ignite with free TP backup
- Currently only Akshan, Kayle, Quinn, Malphite, Sion, and Tahm Kench have Ignite — several more matchups should consider it

### 4. Boots of Swiftness Should Be Default

Every data source shows Boots of Swiftness as the highest WR and most picked boot:
- Mobafire: 54% WR, 68% pickrate
- MetaSRC, Mobalytics, Hof's guide: All recommend Swifties as default

The guide currently lists various boots per matchup but should note Swifties as the default with situational exceptions.

### 5. Phase Rush vs Conqueror — Largely Correct

The matchup-specific keystone choices are mostly correct. Conqueror is most popular overall but Phase Rush is taken situationally. The guide's Phase Rush matchups (Darius, Illaoi, Nasus, Mordekaiser, K'Sante, Renekton, Camille, Fiora, etc.) align with OTP usage.
