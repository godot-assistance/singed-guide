## Example 1: Darius (Hard Matchup)

### ❌ BEFORE (Original)
```
Runes: "Phase Rush - MANDATORY. You need it to escape his pull and stacks. 
Legend: Tenacity for his W slow."

Rune Setup:
- Primary: Sorcery (Phase Rush, Nimbus Cloak, Celerity, Gathering Storm)
- Secondary: Precision (Triumph, Legend: Tenacity)
- Shards: Adaptive Force, Adaptive Force, Tenacity
```

### ✅ AFTER (Updated)
```
Runes: "Phase Rush - NON-NEGOTIABLE vs Darius. You NEED it to escape after 
he E pulls you (only way to survive). Nimbus Cloak + Celerity + Ghost = 
maximum escape speed. Second Wind + Doran's Shield for sustain against his Q 
poke. Unflinching reduces his W slow (45% slow is deadly). TENACITY SHARD 
(not Health) - every 0.1s less slowed = more distance escaped. Armor shard 
mandatory - he does pure physical. NO Attack Speed - you're not fighting him, 
you're surviving."

Rune Setup:
- Primary: Sorcery (Phase Rush, Nimbus Cloak, Celerity, Gathering Storm)
- Secondary: Resolve (Second Wind, Unflinching)
- Shards: Adaptive Force, Armor, Tenacity
- Starting Item: Doran's Shield (not Dark Seal)
```

### 📈 Key Improvements:
1. **Secondary tree change:** Precision → Resolve (sustain vs poke)
2. **Added Second Wind:** Mandatory sustain vs Q poke
3. **Added Unflinching:** Stacks with tenacity for 60%+ CC reduction
4. **Shard optimization:** Armor shard (he does physical damage)
5. **Strategic depth:** Explains WHY every choice, HOW to survive
6. **Starting item:** Doran's Shield mandatory (not Dark Seal)

**Result:** Went from "take Phase Rush" to complete survival strategy

1. **Keystone change:** Conqueror → Arcane Comet (unique choice!)
2. **Philosophy shift:** All-in build → Poke/proxy optimization
3. **Added Scorch:** Early burn damage amplifier
4. **Transcendence:** 20% CDR at level 11 for more proxying
5. **Presence of Mind:** Mana sustain for extended proxy
6. **Strategic reasoning:** WHY Comet works better than Conqueror

**Result:** Went from generic to strategically unique
