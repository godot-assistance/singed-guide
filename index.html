🧪 General Singed Wisdom

🏃 Proxy Farming

Run between enemy towers and farm the wave there. Forces enemy to choose between tower and farm. High risk, high reward.

🌊 Fling Priority

Fling carries into your team, not tanks. Flip them into tower or poison trail. Timing > Distance.

💨 Poison Management

Your Q costs mana over time. Toggle it strategically. Don't leave it on when farming safely under tower.

🛡️ Core Philosophy

Singed is a disruptor, not a duelist. Your job: Run around like a madman, split pressure, draw attention, never die.

⚔️ Core Builds

Standard Build

Liandry's Anguish Rylai's Crystal Scepter Demonic Embrace

Core burn build. Run around, poison everything, never die. Classic Singed.

Tank Singed

Heartsteel Jak'Sho Thornmail

Against heavy AD. You become unkillable. Less damage, more chaos.

Proxy Build

Dark Seal (x2) Corrupting Potion Boots

Early game proxy setup. Stack Dark Seals, never back, live between their towers.
