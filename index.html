🧪 General Singed Wisdom

🏃 Proxy Farming

Run between enemy towers and farm the wave there. Forces enemy to choose between tower and farm. High risk, high reward.

🌊 Fling Priority

Fling carries into your team, not tanks. Flip them into tower or poison trail. Timing > Distance.

💨 Poison Management

Your Q costs mana over time. Toggle it strategically. Don't leave it on when farming safely under tower.

🛡️ Core Philosophy

Singed is a disruptor, not a duelist. Your job: Run around like a madman, split pressure, draw attention, never die.

⚔️ Core Builds (S16 Patch 26.3)

Standard Build

Liandry's Torment Rylai's Crystal Scepter Deadman's Plate

The build. Liandry's is your ONLY true core item. Rylai's for perma slow. Deadman's for armor + zoom. Works in 90% of games.

AP-Heavy (Solo Carry)

Liandry's Torment Bloodletter's Curse Rabadon's Deathcap

When your team needs damage. Deathcap rush more important in S16 due to AP nerfs across items.

Movespeed / SHTF Build

Cosmic Drive Deadman's Plate Liandry's Torment

When lane is doomed. Rush Aether Wisp + Winged Moonplate + Swifties. Perma proxy and run away. You can't die if they can't catch you.

🛡️ Tank / Survival Build

Liandry's Torment Protoplasm Harness Rylai's Crystal Scepter

When you're getting focused or need to survive. Protoplasm Lifeline passive is turbo broken on Singed — 600 HP + clutch heal when you're running through 5 people. Build this when behind or vs heavy burst (Camille, Fiora, Irelia, Riven, Yone). Minishcap1 has a video called "PROTOPLASM IS BROKEN" — it's not clickbait, it's just facts. Mobalytics standard build across 35k matches. Bandlepipes (2300g) is also cracked as a cheap hybrid option if enemy team is mixed damage and you need to not explode.

📜 Rune Setups

Conqueror (Standard)

Primary: Conqueror, Triumph, Legend: Alacrity, Last Stand
Secondary: Nimbus Cloak, Celerity

Best 5v5 damage. Take vs tanks and most melee. Stack with poison + fling + auto.

Phase Rush (Mandatory vs Slows)

Primary: Phase Rush, Nimbus Cloak, Celerity, Gathering Storm
Secondary: Second Wind, Unflinching

MANDATORY vs Darius, Olaf, Nasus. Also good for proxy escapes. If you don't know what to take, Phase Rush is always safe.
